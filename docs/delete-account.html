Delete your data or your account

Both are done from inside the app, in a few taps. You never need to email anyone.

Tee gives you two separate controls, because they do different things. Read the table below before choosing.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Tee and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. Your rounds, scores, saved courses and clubs are erased immediately.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Tee and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Everything above is erased, your account record is removed, and you are signed out.

This cannot be undone.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no grace period and no way for us to restore an account afterwards — we do not keep a copy. If you only want to stop using Tee for a while, simply sign out instead.

One thing that is deliberately kept

If you pinned the location of a green while playing, that pin stays on the shared course map. Greens are contributed to a common map so nobody has to map the same hole twice, and removing yours would break the distance readout for every other golfer at that course.

The pin is a coordinate attached to a golf hole. It carries no name, no account link and nothing that identifies you — after deletion there is no way to connect it back to you.

Can't get into the app?

If you have lost access to your account and cannot reach the Settings screen, email ffuensalida@icloud.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you. We will confirm once it is done, normally within a few days.
