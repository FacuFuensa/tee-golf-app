Tee

Know your number.

Tee tells you one thing, fast: how far you are from the green. Open a hole and the number is right there — live GPS distance from where you stand, in yards or metres. No menus, no clutter.

What it does

Live distance

GPS yardage to the green, updated as you walk up to your ball.

Map any course

Drop a pin on each green from the satellite map — from the course or from your sofa.

Keep score

One stepper per hole, running score to par, and a scorecard strip you can scrub.

Play as a group

Share a six-character code. Everyone keeps their own card; the leaderboard is live.

Your bag

Add your clubs and how far you actually carry them. Tee suggests the one that fits.

See the strokes

Scoring average per 18, splits by par 3 / 4 / 5, best round, full history.

Privacy, briefly

No ads. No tracking. No advertising SDK. Tee collects the minimum it needs to work, your location never leaves your phone except to fetch the weather at the course, and you can erase everything from inside the app at any time. The full policy spells out every field.
